# 2026-09-08 AI Agent Identity News

**Summary**: Estonia publicly corrects the premise of its own June announcement — AI agents will **not** receive personal identification codes. The Aruait project's actual direction is delegated, revocable authority with liability retained by the human, backed by a trust registry.

**Sources**: 2026-09-08-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-09-08

## Estonia and Estonia-related coverage

### AI agents will not receive personal identification codes

ERR reports that Estonia will not issue personal identification codes directly to AI agents. The emerging approach instead lets agents act on a person's behalf in government portals while responsibility stays with the human. The Aruait project is focused on legal questions, information security, data protection, authentication, revocable authorization, and zero-trust controls (source: 2026-09-08-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [ERR News in English](https://news.err.ee/1610132113/ai-agents-will-not-get-estonian-id-codes-and-responsibility-will-stay-with-humans) and [ERR Novaator in Estonian](https://novaator.err.ee/1610131744/tehisaruagendid-eesti-isikukoodi-siiski-ei-saa-ja-vastutus-jaab-inimestele), published 2026-09-08).

The project lead characterises the earlier "AI personal code" framing as a communications tactic; the practical goal is to link an agent to the person it represents without treating the agent as a human legal identity (source: 2026-09-08-ai-agent-identity-news.md). This is a **direct correction** of the June 2026 announcement — see [[estonia-ai-agent-id]].

This run also produced the first Estonia-origin material matching the **aruait** keyword, which had returned nothing in the two previous runs.

### Aruait's official scope

RIA describes Aruait as the foundation for safe autonomous AI agents in Estonia's public sector. Planned outputs: an **Identity 2.0** framework for limited, auditable, revocable authority; a public **AI-assistant trust registry**; interoperability standards; and an automated service-to-service pilot (source: 2026-09-08-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [RIA — Reason Reserve / Aruait](https://ria.ee/en/state-information-system/artificial-intelligence/reason-reserve-aruait), last updated 2026-08-28). This is a **primary government source**, which is why it carries more weight than the secondary commentary in the previous report.

### Estonian business commentary on liability

Äritehnoloogia examines whether an AI agent needs an official digital identity or personal code, particularly where it signs contracts, conducts banking transactions, or makes employment-related changes — centring on where executive and human responsibility should remain (source: 2026-09-08-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Äritehnoloogia](https://www.aritehnoloogia.ee/arvamused/2026/09/08/arinoustaja-kui-teie-ai-agent-teeks-homme-vale-kande-kelle-kaest-te-aru-parite), published 2026-09-08). See [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]].
