## Section 2 — Other countries

- **Hong Kong — HKMA Project SAGE (2 Nov; primary)**: HKMA launched Project SAGE and an Agentic Finance Expert Group with four work areas: financial use cases; verification of agent identity and delegated authority (including Know Your Agent and authentication); interoperability standards; and legal questions (governance, liability, consumer protection). A companion announcement confirms publication of a discussion paper on banks' use of agentic AI. Publication hour unavailable, so ordering against the previous cutoff is not conclusive. Not an adopted KYA standard, registry or binding requirement; the discussion paper was not inspected (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [HKMA Project SAGE](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261102-4/) and [HKMA FinTech Week announcement](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261102-3)). See [[china-ai-agent-policy]].
- **United Kingdom — NCSC Cyber Shield (3 Nov, v1.0; primary)**: five-year programme linking defensive agents to national cyber intelligence; NCSC provides the core platform and data fusion, industry builds and operates agents; consultation on protocols, standards and data models; initial focus on detection and blocking, with autonomous remediation needing stronger assurance. Not an agent-ID law or finished standard. The report says the page's indexed text and date were verified but direct retrieval was unavailable (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [NCSC Cyber Shield](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/annual-review-2026/future-cyber-defence/cyber-shield)). See [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]].
- **United Arab Emirates — Abu Dhabi Machine-Readable Government (2 Nov, 09:41 UTC; issuer press release)**: Abu Dhabi Government and OpenAI announced an initiative with a food-import compliance pilot described as in production; automated assessments cite the governing policy clause, officials retain responsibility for final decisions, and autonomous high-stakes determinations affecting rights, eligibility or essential services are excluded. Deployment and performance claims are the issuers' own, not independently validated; concerns delegated decision-making and auditability, not agent codes (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [ZAWYA](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/openai-and-abu-dhabi-government-launch-worlds-first-ai-native-machine-readable-government-initiative-1869353)). See [[united-arab-emirates-ai-agent-policy]].
