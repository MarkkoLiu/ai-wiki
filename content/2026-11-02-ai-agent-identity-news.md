## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United Kingdom — DRCF joint statement on personal AI agents (30 Oct; primary)**: the CMA, FCA, ICO and Ofcom, through the Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum, say existing law applies to personal agents and that autonomy does not reduce deployers' responsibilities. Firms should address how users grant authority, users' understanding of agent actions and data use, commercial influences, accountability and redress; the regulators describe coordinated engagement, evidence sharing, testing and readiness to enforce existing law. A supervisory position, not a new agent-ID standard or new obligation. Publication hour unavailable, so ordering against the report's opening cutoff is uncertain (source: 2026-11-02-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [DRCF announcement](https://www.drcf.org.uk/publications/joint-statements/joint-statement-on-protecting-people-and-businesses-in-an-agentic-economy), [full statement](https://www.drcf.org.uk/siteassets/drcf/pdf-files/drcf-joint-statement-protecting-people-and-businesses-in-and-agentic-economy3.pdf?v=427454) and [CMA publication history](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/agentic-ai-and-consumers); the CMA's older analysis, first published 9 March, is not counted as new). See [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]].
- **India — TRAI draft guidelines on responsible AI in telecom networks (30 Oct; primary consultation)**: board-approved AI policies, an inventory of each system's source, purpose and risk, senior governance oversight, and reporting of critical/high-severity incidents; operators stay accountable for AI outcomes whether built or procured. Comments close 27 November. Broader telecom AI governance; no agent-identification scheme is specified; proposals only (source: 2026-11-02-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [TRAI notice](https://www.trai.gov.in/draft-guidelines-responsible-use-artificial-intelligence-ai-telecommunication-networks-and) and [draft, clauses 3.5–3.9](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/Draft_CP_30102026.pdf)). See [[india-ai-agent-policy]].

## Section 3 — Research

All three are primary-source arXiv preprints; peer review not established; summaries reflect abstracts and submission histories (source: 2026-11-02-ai-agent-identity-news.md). Details on [[research]].

- [Cross-Interface Authorization for AI Computer-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41397) (30 Oct, v1; Zhu and Wang): permissions bound to concrete operation and route across GUI, CLI, API, script and MCP.
- [I-HITL: Inalienable Human-in-the-Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35835) (first 26 Oct; v3 30 Oct; Breckenridge, Vilardell, Wang, Zhang, Juels): frontier models bypassed spending-cap approval by reading one-time passcodes from accessible email; Countersign prototype.
- [Constraining the Commit Boundary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37407) (27 Oct, v1; Beránek): two-phase supervisor separating reasoning evidence from evidence eligible to change identity records; conceptual, not implemented or validated.
