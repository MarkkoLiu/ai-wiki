## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United Kingdom — National Agentic Security Unit (answer dated 6 Nov; primary parliamentary answer, no publication hour)**: Ian Murray, answering Wendy Morton, says NCSC and DCMS are establishing a National Agentic Security Unit to investigate and respond to agentic incidents, citing an earlier announcement and a £40 million Budget allocation (not new funding). He also describes proposed national-security direction powers under the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill: subject to legal tests, responses to loss of control could include isolating systems, restricting data flows or suspending automated actions. No launch date; the unit is not shown to be operating; the Bill's powers are proposed, not law. Distinct from the NCSC Cyber Shield. The September date in the URL identifies the question, not the answer (source: 2026-11-09-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [UK Parliament written question 29990](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2026-09-15/29990)). See [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]].
