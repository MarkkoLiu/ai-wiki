## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United Kingdom — ICO and CMA on Meta Muse's UK launch (24 Nov 2026; primary + secondary)**: the ICO says Meta agreed to **voluntary monitoring and reporting** so the regulator can scrutinise how Muse works and what users experience; the CMA says Meta **adjusted its terms** after discussions and that suppliers should explain what happens when users give an agent more access or autonomy. The ICO separately reminded developers of consumer agentic services to provide privacy protections by default and meaningful user controls, with **further guidance due in spring 2027** (source: 2026-11-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [ICO statement on Meta Muse](https://cy.ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/11/statement-on-uk-launch-of-meta-muse/), [CMA statement](https://competitionandmarkets.blog.gov.uk/2026/11/24/cma-statement-on-uk-launch-of-meta-muse/) and [ICO expectations for consumer agents](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/11/developers-creating-agentic-ai-services-for-consumers-reminded-to-meet-their-data-protection-responsibilities/), primary; [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/privacy-must-be-built-into-consumer-facing-agentic-products-meta-muse-ai-has-officially-launched-in-the-uk-but-british-data-watchdogs-are-keeping-a-close-eye-on-where-your-data-goes), secondary).
  - **Status**: a voluntary arrangement and supervisory expectations, not an enforcement finding, a new rule or an agent-identity scheme. Follows the DRCF letter (16 Nov) calling for compliance before personal-agent launches. See [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]].
- **United Kingdom — FCA expectations for agentic AI in financial services (24 Nov 2026; primary + secondary)**: firms should assess whether what their agents actually do (e.g. payment services, credit broking, financial advice) requires **regulatory authorization**, and consider how they will **identify a customer's agent, verify the customer's consent, determine what the agent may do** and help if something goes wrong. The FCA plans a **policy sprint** with firms, technology providers and policymakers (source: 2026-11-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [FCA agentic AI guidance](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/innovation/ai-approach/agentic-ai), primary, and [Reuters via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/ai-financial-services-trigger-regulation-warning-uk-watchdog), secondary).
  - **Status**: expectations and planned engagement; no agent-identity standard set. A progression from the FCA's 2026-10-19 commitment to a framework on agent identity, delegated authority, accountability and execution. See [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]], [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]].
- **Singapore — PathFin.ai trials of payment mandates (24 Nov 2026; secondary + primary background)**: Green Link Digital Bank, AXS, Terminal 3 and HSBC Singapore described two controlled trials under MAS's **PathFin.ai** initiative — a bill-payment test and a corporate-treasury test — examining how to establish an agent's identity, verify whom it represents and check requested actions against the client's mandate **before** a request reaches a financial institution; the treasury scenario also tested obtaining client confirmation (source: 2026-11-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Fintech News Singapore](https://fintechnews.sg/142186/ai/pathfin-ai-agentic-payments-safeguards-mas/), secondary, and [Terminal 3 via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/terminal-3-partners-with-banks-to-test-safeguards-for-agentic-finance-under-mass-pathfinai-initiative-302948091.html), primary background).
  - **Status**: controlled trials, not a deployed or mandated scheme. The report calls this implementation detail for a "previously reported safeguards framework"; this wiki records MAS's SAFR programme (2026-11-17/19) but had not recorded PathFin.ai by name, so the precise relationship **needs verification**. The secondary article's publication hour is unverified (source: 2026-11-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md). See [[singapore-ai-agent-policy]].
