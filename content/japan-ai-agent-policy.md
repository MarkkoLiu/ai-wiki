# Japan: AI Agent Policy

**Summary**: Japan's Digital Agency has opened council-level work (22 October 2026) on how existing law treats AI agents that express intent, represent users and conclude contracts, tied to permissions, authentication and audit records.

**Sources**: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-10-25

---

## 2026-10-22: AI and Digital Reform Promotion Council, first meeting

- A draft issues paper proposes clarifying how existing law applies to agents expressing intent, representing users and concluding contracts — including contracts exceeding the user's intention. It links the legal analysis to permissions, authentication and logs, and proposes system-level safeguards: agent harnesses, identity authentication, guardrails, authority management, evaluation and audit records (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Material 4, pp. 4–6](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/1f3aa34a-786d-4b56-99a4-469f8f933f1c/4296e96c/20261022_meeting_ai-digital_docs_04.pdf) and [council page](https://www.digital.go.jp/councils/ai_digital/1f3aa34a-786d-4b56-99a4-469f8f933f1c); primary).
- A member submission (Emiko Maruyama) examines attribution of agent actions, allocation of unexpected losses and the records needed to identify responsibility (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Material 9](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/1f3aa34a-786d-4b56-99a4-469f8f933f1c/249dab72/20261022_meeting_ai-digital_docs_09.pdf); primary).
- **Status**: proposals for deliberation — not enacted agent-personhood rules or binding identity requirements.

Japan was previously named only in passing as a comparator in the OECD working-group article (see [[estonia-ai-agent-id]]); this is the first Japanese primary source in the wiki.
