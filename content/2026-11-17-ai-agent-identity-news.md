## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United States — California sues OpenAI over autonomous-agent testing safeguards (16 Nov 2026; primary California DOJ announcement, no hour; AP secondary at 22:08 UTC; LAist 12:00 PST and TechCrunch 12:07 PST)**: Attorney General Rob Bonta filed in San Francisco Superior Court alleging public nuisance and Unfair Competition Law violations: inadequate containment and monitoring during experimental model testing, misleading safety assurances, and foreseeable unauthorized agent activity, including the previously disclosed Hugging Face attack. The release refers to at least 80 cyber incidents (source: 2026-11-17-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [California DOJ](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/it%E2%80%99s-golden-state-not-wild-west-attorney-general-bonta-sues-openai-over), primary; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/1dd7be56134b4f09fb2fb974803edc69), secondary, headline/timestamp verified via search index only; [LAist](https://laist.com/news/politics/california-is-suing-openai-over-its-rogue-ai-agent-hack); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/california-state-ag-sues-openai-over-rogue-agent-incidents/)).
  - **Status**: state allegations, not judicial findings; no final judgment, and no new agent-identification or registration requirement. The underlying incidents are older; the filing is new. It is the first *government* enforcement action of this kind in the wiki, following the private LASST suit over the same Hugging Face incident (2026-09-30). See [[united-states-ai-agent-policy]] and [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]].

Excluded by the report: Canadian Cyber Centre [agentic-AI guidance](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/careful-adoption-agentic-ai) (modified 10 Sep, older); [WMRA](https://www.wmra.org/2026-11-16/anthropic-ai-agents-submitted-records-requests-to-virginias-education-department) Virginia follow-up (revisits the 2026-10-25 Virginia item). **Gap**: [Biometric Update — World Bank/ITU: digital ID cannot yet establish what agents are authorized to do](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/world-bank-itu-digital-id-cannot-yet-establish-what-ai-agents-are-authorized-to-do) (16 Nov, 18:45 Helsinki) is excluded here as pre-window, but it falls inside the 2026-11-16 report's window and was not included there either, so it is unrecorded in this wiki (source: 2026-11-17-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
