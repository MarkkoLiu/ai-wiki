## Section 2 — Other countries

### United Kingdom: open letter on governing agentic AI

Published 5 October 2026 (letter dated 3 October) by Professor Nick Jennings, Loughborough University's vice-chancellor. It is a **primary institutional policy proposal, not adopted government policy or a standard** (source: 2026-10-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Loughborough University open letter](https://blog.lboro.ac.uk/vice-chancellor/2026/10/05/open-letter-governing-agentic-ai-so-that-responsibility-remains-clear/); publication time verified via [public post metadata](https://blog.lboro.ac.uk/vice-chancellor/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/840)).

It asks the UK government to clarify legal responsibility for agents acting across services, pilot significant-incident reporting and independent investigation, and introduce agent-specific public procurement requirements (an accountable deployer; records of consequential actions and their authority; bounded access and spending; tested human intervention). It also proposes interoperable standards under which agents identify themselves, prove whom they represent, and show permissions that can expire or be revoked. Details are on [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]], [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]] and [[agent-standards-and-interoperability]].
