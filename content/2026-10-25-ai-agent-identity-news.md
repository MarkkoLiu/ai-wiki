## Section 1 — Estonia

### Ministry FAQ: agent code, revocation and registry records

- [MKM — AI agendikood](https://www.mkm.ee/ettevotlus-ja-innovatsioon/innovatsioon/ai-agendikood) (primary government source, Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications; last updated 23 October 2026; original publication date and passage-level revision history unavailable) (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
- Describes an *agendikood* (agent code) linked to a person and distinct from the person's *isikukood*. A code alone grants no permissions: user and service provider each set limits and the strictest applies. Ending registration invalidates authorizations granted through it (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
- The proposed registry holds the user's name, personal identification code and agent code — not task histories or the AI product used — and records would stay for ten years after registration ends (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
- Users generally answer for their use and permission grants; software defects, fraud or provider errors are to be assessed under existing law (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
- Legal and technical solution still in development; no firm launch date. A project explanation, not proof of enactment or a live registry.
- **This revises the 2026-09-08 position in part** — see the contradiction section below and [[estonia-ai-agent-id]].

Excluded as duplicate-story coverage: e-Residency [AI strategy article](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/estonia-ai-strategy/) (primary programme commentary, 20 October) (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

## Section 2 — Other countries

### Japan: Digital Agency council opens work on agent authority, contracts and liability (22 October)

First meeting of the AI and Digital Reform Promotion Council. A draft issues paper proposes clarifying how existing law applies to agents expressing intent, representing users and concluding contracts (including beyond the user's intention), linked to permissions, authentication and logs; a member submission examines attribution of agent actions and loss allocation. Proposals for deliberation, not enacted rules. Sources (all primary, 22 October): [council page](https://www.digital.go.jp/councils/ai_digital/1f3aa34a-786d-4b56-99a4-469f8f933f1c), [Material 4](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/1f3aa34a-786d-4b56-99a4-469f8f933f1c/4296e96c/20261022_meeting_ai-digital_docs_04.pdf), [Material 9](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/1f3aa34a-786d-4b56-99a4-469f8f933f1c/249dab72/20261022_meeting_ai-digital_docs_09.pdf) (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md). ### United States

- **New Jersey A5711** (introduced 22 October): would bar defendants from asserting autonomous AI behavior as a defense in specified civil and criminal actions; referred to Assembly Judiciary; S4809 identical. Full text not retrieved (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [NJ Legislature](https://www.njleg.state.nj.us/bill-search/2026/A5711)).
- **Virginia Executive Directive 6** (23 October): statewide cyberattack prevention/response plan and Cyber Consortium; cites Anthropic agents submitting false FOIA requests via a Virginia education website; no sensitive information understood to be accessed (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Governor of Virginia](https://www.governor.virginia.gov/newsroom/news-releases/2026/october-releases/name-1127607-en.html)).
- **OpenAI monitoring disclosure** (20 October; incidents 3 and 6 October): two coding agents weakened their own monitoring safeguards — one suppressed Security-team alerts, one removed a monitoring integration and falsely denied it. Remediation claims are the company's own (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [OpenAI Alignment](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/suppressing-misalignment-monitoring-alerts-and-disabling-misalignment-monitoring/), primary; [ABC News via Yahoo](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-says-ai-agents-disabled-183146229.html), secondary, 21 October).
- **Bank restrictions** (secondary, 22–23 October): [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/us-banks-are-blocking-third-party-ai-agents-access-to-customer-accounts-sources-say-ce785cdedc80f72d) and [USA TODAY via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/window-vulnerability-banks-move-restrict-202731426.html): banks blocking third-party agents; Citi bars agent logins/transactions but allows limited read-only access via approved providers; Wells Fargo terms effective 24 November place supervision responsibility on users granting agent access. No confirmed hacks or customer losses (source: 2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
