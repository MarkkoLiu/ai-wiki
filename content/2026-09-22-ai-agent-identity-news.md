## Section 2 — Other countries

### United States: Amazon blocks Meta's Muse shopping agent

On 21 September, Amazon blocked Meta's Muse personal AI agent from browsing customer accounts and buying products on Amazon. Amazon said Meta had not disclosed that Muse would access its store or obtained Amazon's authorization; the agent did not identify itself while browsing and could reach account information and process transactions using customer credentials held in secure storage. Amazon called Muse an undisclosed third party and said agents buying on users' behalf should operate openly and respect whether a service provider chooses to participate. Meta did not comment to the cited reporters (source: 2026-09-22-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/21/amazon-meta-muse-ai-agentic-shopping) and [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/metas-ai-agent-has-been-blocked-from-using-amazon-com/), 2026-09-21, secondary reporting based on Amazon's statements).

Note on sourcing: both accounts rest on Amazon's statements; Meta's side is not represented. Claims about what Muse could access are **Amazon's characterisation** and need verification against a Meta or independent source.

Added to [[united-states-ai-agent-policy]] and [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]].
