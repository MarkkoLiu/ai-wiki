## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United Kingdom — Commons agrees the AI-agent clause; Lords–Commons exchange continues (vote 24 Nov, papers 25 Nov; primary)**: in the 24 November debate the Commons agreed to **Lords amendment 33** to the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill after voting **278–93** against a proposal to broaden its wording from "AI agents" to "autonomous systems". The clause gives the Secretary of State functions where **loss of oversight or control of an AI agent** may compromise a relevant network or information system; definitions are left to regulations and the government plans to consult on implementation. On 25 November Parliament published the Commons' reasons for **disagreeing with Lords amendments 107 and 108** (not the agent clause), returning those disputes to the Lords (source: 2026-11-26-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Parliamentary bill record](https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/4035), [Commons disagreement and reasons](https://bills.parliament.uk/publications/69022/documents/9119) and [Commons Hansard, 24 Nov](https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/2026-11-24/debates/9696CEE9-A8C3-449D-9B15-89FA9F00CF8E/CyberSecurityAndResilience%28NetworkAndInformationSystems%29Bill), primary).
  - **Status**: the bill remains at consideration of amendments; no Royal Assent and no operative regulations. The vote itself predates the window; the report includes it via the newly published procedural papers. Exact release hour of the 25 Nov document unavailable (source: 2026-11-26-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
  - **Relation to earlier entries**: this is the Commons stage of the clause the Lords agreed on 26 Oct, recorded on [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]] as **"Amendment 70"**. The report numbers it **amendment 33**; amendment numbers are normally reassigned when Lords amendments are sent to the Commons, so this is most likely a renumbering, not a different clause — but the report does not state the link, so the identity of the two **needs verification**. No contradiction.

No other qualifying, non-duplicate country-level development was verified (source: 2026-11-26-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
