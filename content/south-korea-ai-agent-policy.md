# South Korea: AI Agent Policy

**Summary**: South Korea appears so far only through a corporate disclosure: SK Telecom's first AI Governance Report (26 October 2026), describing employee-ID-numbered agents with named human supervisors. No Korean government policy is yet covered in this wiki.

**Sources**: 2026-10-26-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-10-26

---

## 2026-10-26: SK Telecom AI Governance Report

- Agents reportedly receive employee ID numbers and designated human supervisors; roles, activity and performance are tracked; higher-autonomy or higher-impact agents need a more senior supervisor (source: 2026-10-26-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/technology/2026/10/26/sk-telecom-issues-first-ai-governance-report), secondary).
- A lifecycle framework — identification, access control, monitoring, evaluation, suspension or retirement — is described as developing; some elements are future plans (source: 2026-10-26-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [SK Telecom newsroom](https://news.sktelecom.com/232386), primary, Korean; full page not inspected).
- **Status**: an organizational model linking agent identity to human responsibility. Not a government-issued code, legal personhood or a national legal requirement. Details rest on newspaper reporting, not the full report (needs verification against the primary report).
