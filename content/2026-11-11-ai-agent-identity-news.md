## Section 1 — Estonia

- **Cabinet discusses legal basis for agent-code registration (10 Nov 2026, 10:25; primary government source — cabinet agenda and explanation, Government Communication Office)**: item 4 of the cabinet meeting concerned a draft amendment to Public Information Act § 43(10) on agent-code registration. The explanation describes a unique code linked to a person, letting services identify when an AI agent acts on that person's behalf, with transparency, security and responsibility as objectives (source: 2026-11-11-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Government of Estonia — Cabinet meeting agenda, 10 November 2026, item 4](https://valitsus.ee/uudised/valitsuse-kabinetinoupidamise-paevakord-10-november-2026)).
- **Status**: cabinet-level *discussion* only. The source does not establish cabinet approval of a final bill, submission to the Riigikogu, parliamentary passage, commencement or a live registry. It does not establish that agents receive human personal identification codes (isikukood), or that the code alone grants authority to act (source: 2026-11-11-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
- **Relation to earlier coverage**: this is the first dated cabinet-level step beyond the [MKM agent-code FAQ](https://www.mkm.ee/ettevotlus-ja-innovatsioon/innovatsioon/ai-agendikood) (see [[2026-10-25-ai-agent-identity-news]]) and the Tallinn declaration ([[2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news]]). It is consistent with the person-linked *agendikood* model on [[estonia-ai-agent-id]] and does not reopen the 2026-09-08 isikukood correction.

No other relevant, non-duplicate Estonia development was verified (source: 2026-11-11-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United States / international standards ecosystem — Agent Name Service launches under Linux Foundation governance (10 Nov 2026, 11:00 US Eastern; primary issuer press release via PR Newswire, not independent reporting)**: ANS uses DNS and certificate-based signals for agent identity, verification and discovery. Its seven-document specification covers naming and registration, versioning, DNS publication, log auditing, integrity monitoring and agent-to-agent authentication, governed by a technical steering committee. The release reports eleven supporting organisations and early interoperability work with AGNTCY and Agentic Resource Discovery. It formalises a June announcement of intent (source: 2026-11-11-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Linux Foundation — Agent Name Service launch, via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/linux-foundation-welcomes-agent-name-service-to-bring-verifiable-identity-and-discovery-to-ai-agents-302935151.html)).
- **Status**: not a government-issued personal-code scheme, a statutory requirement, or a final IETF/ISO standard. Adoption and interoperability claims are the issuer's and were not independently tested. Establishing an agent's identity does not by itself establish delegated permission or legal accountability (source: 2026-11-11-ai-agent-identity-news.md). See [[agent-standards-and-interoperability]] and [[ai-agent-identity]].
