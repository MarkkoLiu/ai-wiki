# China: AI Agent Identity Policy

**Summary**: China reportedly introduced a national standard for AI-agent interconnection that includes unified identity management and identity-code provisions for AI agents (2026-06-26).

**Sources**: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-09-04

---

China reportedly introduced a national standard for AI-agent interconnection that includes unified identity management and identity-code provisions for AI agents (source: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/policy/article/3358559/digital-id-cards-china-moves-regulate-ai-agents-unified-identity-system), published 2026-06-26). This reporting is secondary, citing Chinese official and state-media sources (source: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

This is described in the source as a country-level development closely comparable to [[estonia-ai-agent-id|Estonia's proposed AI-agent personal identification codes]] (source: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
