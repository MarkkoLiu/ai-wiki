## Section 1 — Estonia

- **RIA Aruait update** (primary; updated 15 October). Distinguishes Eesti.ai's AI-identity initiative (how a user grants authority) from Aruait (how public-service providers verify that authority and permit actions). Task-limited access, purpose-limited data use, revocation that prevents further actions, traceability of actions and their legal authority, human intervention and error correction. Outputs: evaluate existing trust and interoperability solutions and test a complete service process incl. when human approval or signature is required. **New relative to earlier wiki**: the older English page described a planned central trust registry and Identity 2.0; the current Estonian wording does *not* establish that the registry was cancelled or that new legal powers took effect, and says the market-consultation date is undecided (source: 2026-10-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [RIA — Aruait](https://ria.ee/riigi-infosusteem/tehisaru/aruait)).
- **ERR legal commentary — Sten Tikerpe** (secondary opinion, 15 October 13:34 Helsinki). Reports that the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications seeks interministerial coordination on a **digital-identity bill by 22 October**; argues the draft leaves the identity's meaning, legal effects, costs and safeguards undefined and that explanatory material cannot substitute for rules in the law; recommends withholding approval. The author's assessment only — not a court ruling or official rejection. The government draft could not be retrieved, so the 22 October deadline is **unconfirmed from a primary record** (source: 2026-10-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [ERR — Sten Tikerpe](https://www.err.ee/1610166907/sten-tikerpe-ti-agentidele-isikukoodide-andmise-eelnou-on-taiesti-toores)). **Tension flagged**: the article's headline frames the bill as one "giving AI agents ID codes", which sits uneasily with the 2026-09-08 position that agents will not receive personal identification codes. See [[estonia-ai-agent-id]] for how this is handled.
