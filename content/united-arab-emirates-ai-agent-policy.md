# United Arab Emirates — AI Agent Policy

**Summary**: Abu Dhabi Government and OpenAI's Machine-Readable Government initiative: automated compliance decisions linked to governing rules, with human officials retaining final responsibility. No agent-identity scheme.

**Sources**: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-11-04

---

## Abu Dhabi Machine-Readable Government (2 November 2026)

Abu Dhabi Government and OpenAI announced the initiative, with a food-import compliance pilot described as already in production. Automated assessments link to the precise policy clause supporting an outcome; officials oversee the system and retain responsibility for final decisions. The release explicitly excludes autonomous high-stakes determinations materially affecting individuals' rights, eligibility or essential services (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Abu Dhabi Government/OpenAI release via ZAWYA](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/openai-and-abu-dhabi-government-launch-worlds-first-ai-native-machine-readable-government-initiative-1869353), issuer press release, 09:41 UTC).

## Status and limits

Relevant to delegated decision-making, auditability and accountability (see [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]]), not to agent-identification codes. Deployment and performance descriptions are issuer claims; no independent validation or change to underlying law is established (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md). Not an agent-ID scheme.
