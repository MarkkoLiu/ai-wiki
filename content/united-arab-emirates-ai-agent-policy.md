# United Arab Emirates — AI Agent Policy

**Summary**: Abu Dhabi Government and OpenAI's Machine-Readable Government initiative: automated compliance decisions linked to governing rules, with human officials retaining final responsibility. No agent-identity scheme. 2026-11-23: the federal government announced the AI concierge Mira for public services (identity-document renewals with in-conversation user confirmation), first services due March 2027.

**Sources**: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, 2026-11-24-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-11-24

---

## Abu Dhabi Machine-Readable Government (2 November 2026)

Abu Dhabi Government and OpenAI announced the initiative, with a food-import compliance pilot described as already in production. Automated assessments link to the precise policy clause supporting an outcome; officials oversee the system and retain responsibility for final decisions. The release explicitly excludes autonomous high-stakes determinations materially affecting individuals' rights, eligibility or essential services (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Abu Dhabi Government/OpenAI release via ZAWYA](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/openai-and-abu-dhabi-government-launch-worlds-first-ai-native-machine-readable-government-initiative-1869353), issuer press release, 09:41 UTC).

## Status and limits

Relevant to delegated decision-making, auditability and accountability (see [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]]), not to agent-identification codes. Deployment and performance descriptions are issuer claims; no independent validation or change to underlying law is established (source: 2026-11-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md). Not an agent-ID scheme.

## 2026-11-23: federal AI concierge Mira announced

The UAE government unveiled **Mira**, a federal AI concierge to help users complete public-service transactions in a conversation. Ministers demonstrated passport, Emirates ID and residency renewals; the government says users can review details and confirm requests within the conversation where the service allows. The first public services are scheduled for **March 2027** (source: 2026-11-24-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Dubai Protocol Department](https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/mohammed-bin-rashid-witnesses-launch-of-new-uae-government-services-model-ai-concierge-agent-mira/), primary, and [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/23/uae-unveils-government-ai-assistant-called-mira-to-boost-public-services/), secondary).

- **Status and limits**: an announced rollout, not a live nationwide agent service or a new legal delegation standard (source: 2026-11-24-ai-agent-identity-news.md). The in-conversation review-and-confirm step is a user-confirmation control relevant to [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]]; no agent-identity code or registry is described. Federal-level, distinct from the Abu Dhabi initiative above. See [[2026-11-24-ai-agent-identity-news]].
