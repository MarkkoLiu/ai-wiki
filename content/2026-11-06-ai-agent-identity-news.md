## Section 1 — Estonia

- **Tallinn declaration signed (5 Nov, 19:43; primary ministry announcement)**: Estonia, Canada, Israel, Mexico, New Zealand, Portugal, South Korea, the United Kingdom and Brazil signed the Digital Nations declaration "Agentic State: Going Beyond the Hype"; Uruguay is described as joining later. Emphasis on transparency, traceability and human supervision with public authorities retaining final responsibility; the minister says public-sector agents must have an identifiable natural person and clearly defined responsibility behind their actions. Identity and trust, common standards and interoperability are focus areas. A signed intergovernmental declaration, not an enacted agent-code law or live registry; it relies on the ministry's account (the DOCX declaration was not reviewed); no new personal-identification-code requirement (source: 2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs](https://www.justdigi.ee/uudised/fotod-uheksa-digiriigi-juhi-avaldus-tallinnas-tehisaru-arendamisel-peab-inimene-alati-jaama)). This resolves the open question left by [[2026-11-05-ai-agent-identity-news]] (summit scheduled, signing unverified). See [[estonia-ai-agent-id]].
- **Minister Pakosta at Tallinn Digital Summit (5 Nov, 12:18; primary ministry account)**: public-sector agents restricted to explicitly permitted actions; AI will not be given the right to apply a person's digital signature on their behalf; deployment tied to strict risk assessment, sovereignty and human control. Stuart Russell advocated compulsory pre-release testing. Reported positions and intended controls, not an enacted rule or proof of technical enforcement (source: 2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Ministry](https://www.justdigi.ee/uudised/liisa-pakosta-tallinn-digital-summiti-avapaneelis-kasutame-tehisaru-selleks-et-inimene-ei)).

## Section 2 — Other countries

- **Singapore — interoperable agent verification as work in progress (5 Nov; primary speech, no publication hour)**: Jasmin Lau (MDDI) says businesses need to know an agent's identity, permitted actions and who is responsible for failures; MAS and IMDA are working with industry to test whether existing internet infrastructure can support agent verification and accountability; she warns against incompatible sector-specific frameworks; regional agentic-AI testing and standards work is planned for Singapore's 2027 ASEAN chairmanship. Exploratory, explicitly unfinished; no national agent registry or mandatory standard (source: 2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [MDDI](https://www.mddi.gov.sg/newsroom/remarks-by-acting-minister-for-manpower-and-senior-minister-of-state-for-digital-development-and-information-jasmin-lau-at-the-iseas-41st-asean-roundtable-2026/)). Secondary coverage of a Singapore "registry" was used only as a lead and its registry claims were not imported. See [[singapore-ai-agent-policy]].
- **China / Malaysia — Zetrix and CAICT Know-Your-Agent pilot (5 Nov; issuer announcement, no hour)**: a KYA service linking verifiable agent identities to authenticated individuals or organizations, with scoped revocable permissions, limits on onward delegation and signed activity records; a planned pilot would test cross-border recognition across China, ASEAN, the Middle East and Africa while keeping local trust decisions; participants remain subject to confirmation. Findings intended for ITU-T SG17's Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI. Issuer claims unvalidated; not an adopted ITU/IEEE standard or national mandate (source: 2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Zetrix](https://www.zetrix.com/zetrix-ai-and-china-academy-of-information-and-communications-technology-unveil-know-your-agent-kya-service-to-bring-trust-and-accountability-to-ai-agents/)). See [[china-ai-agent-policy]], [[malaysia-ai-agent-policy]], [[agent-standards-and-interoperability]].
