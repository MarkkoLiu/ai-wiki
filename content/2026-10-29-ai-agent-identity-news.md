## Section 2 — Other countries

- **United States — CFTC chair on agent identity, registration and liability (28 Oct; primary regulator speech; exact publication hour unavailable)**: At the inaugural Frontier Forum in Washington (keynote 13:00–13:15 per the official agenda), CFTC Chairman Michael S. Selig asked whether developers of autonomous trading agents should register, who is liable when an agent exceeds its intended role, and how regulators can identify the user represented by an agent or group of agents. He floated "know-your-agent" checks, said the approach should weigh functions performed, control exercised and economic substance, and named correlated trading behaviour and surveillance as risks. This is a discussion, not an adopted registration requirement, proposed rule or standard; Selig said his views do not necessarily reflect the Commission's (source: 2026-10-29-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [CFTC — Remarks at Frontier Forum](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/SpeechesTestimony/opaselig14); [official agenda](https://www.cftc.gov/node/260496)).
