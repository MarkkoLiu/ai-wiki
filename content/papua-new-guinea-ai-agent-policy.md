# Papua New Guinea: AI Agent Policy

**Summary**: Papua New Guinea's Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) released a revised draft National AI Policy (announced 26 November 2026) that would require government AI agents to be identifiable, owned by a named human, and limited to documented permissions, with responsibility resting on the deploying organisation. Consultation draft only.

**Sources**: 2026-11-27-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-11-27

---

## 2026-11-26: revised draft National AI Policy

DICT released a revised consultation draft of the National AI Policy (draft dated 9 Nov 2026; release announced 26 Nov) (source: 2026-11-27-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [DICT release announcement](https://www.ict.gov.pg/dict-sets-direction-for-safe-responsible-use-of-artificail-intelligence-with-revised-national-ai-policy/) and [Revised National AI Policy consultation draft, pp. 15–16 and 21](https://www.ict.gov.pg/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/PNG_National_AI_Policy_Revised_09Nov2026-1.pdf), primary). For government agents the draft would require (same source):

- **Identity and ownership**: agents must be identifiable and have a **named human owner**.
- **Bounded authority**: a defined purpose and **documented permissions**; delegated actions must stay within the original authority.
- **Human approval**: required for high-impact or out-of-scope actions; the proposed initial government pilot requires explicit user approval before **each** external action or change to an official record.
- **Audit and stop**: audit records, plus a way to stop connected agents and revoke access.
- **Responsibility**: assigned to the **deploying organisation**.

Status: consultation draft; DICT said consultation details would follow. Not adopted policy and not legislation. The announcement gives a date but no hour, so its placement against the report's opening cutoff is uncertain (source: 2026-11-27-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

In kind, this combines an identity requirement (identifiable agent, named owner) with bounded delegation — closer to the Tallinn declaration's "named natural person behind public-sector agents" ([[estonia-ai-agent-id]], 2026-11-05) and Kenya's bounded-authorization platform ([[kenya-ai-agent-policy]]) than to a registry or identity-code scheme. See [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]] and [[ai-agent-identity]].
