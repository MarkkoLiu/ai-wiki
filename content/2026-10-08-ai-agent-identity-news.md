## Section 2 — Other countries

### United Kingdom: ICO agentic-AI call for evidence and enquiries into agent testing

Published 8 October 2026; consultation open 8 October to 20 November 2026. **Primary** regulator announcement and consultation notice (source: 2026-10-08-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [ICO news release](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/10/ico-secures-changes-from-leading-ai-developers-as-scrutiny-extends-to-ai-agents/) and [ICO call for evidence](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/ico-and-stakeholder-consultations/2026/10/agentic-ai-call-for-evidence/)).

The Information Commissioner's Office seeks input on applying data protection law to agentic AI — data security, transparency, accountability, automated decision-making, fairness and purpose limitation, lawful processing — to inform final guidance. It also confirmed recent enquiries with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and the UK AI Security Institute about agentic-AI testing and deployment; the announcement reports no findings of wrongdoing. It does not create an agent-identity scheme or adopted authorization standard. Details on [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]] and [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]].
