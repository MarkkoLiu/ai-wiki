## Section 3 — Research

Three primary preprint/journal items (peer review not established for the preprints); details on [[research]]:

- [Approve What, Exactly?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21063) — Kodathala, Pollishetty, Cargill (arXiv 2610.21063, 15 Oct). Audit of ten runtimes found no third-party-verifiable approval record and post-approval argument changes in four of eight where measurable; Agent Step-Up gateway binds a passkey signature to a canonical action digest (source: 2026-10-19-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

- [Risk for AI Agents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-journal-of-risk-regulation/article/risk-for-ai-agents/391CA197A65142C15C32DC69470E98A2) — Faroldi, *European Journal of Risk Regulation* (28 Sept; DOI 10.1017/err.2026.10152). Conceptual argument that general agents need governance beyond product-style risk assessment (source: 2026-10-19-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
