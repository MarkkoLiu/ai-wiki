## Section 1 — Estonia

- **Riigikogu accepts bill 1092 SE into proceedings (17 Nov 2026; primary; no publication hour, so ordering against the window's opening cutoff is unconfirmed)**: the Riigikogu board accepted bill **1092 SE** (supplementing the Public Information Act — agent code) into proceedings and designated the **Constitutional Committee** as lead committee. The government initiated the bill on **16 November**. The amendment would create a legal basis for an AI agent code linked to a person, so that an agent's actions on that person's behalf can be identified (source: 2026-11-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Riigikogu — bills accepted into proceedings](https://www.riigikogu.ee/pressiteated/menetlusse-voeti-eelnou-toostusheite-seaduse-muutmiseks/), primary, agent-code paragraph within a multi-bill announcement; and the [bill 1092 SE record](https://www.riigikogu.ee/tegevus/eelnoud/3257efe0-f7c9-42c1-bf63-bb9b4c5188ec/avaliku-teabe-seaduse-taiendamise-seadus-agendikood/), primary, initiation dated 16 Nov).
  - **Status**: a parliamentary-process step only — not passage, promulgation or commencement, not automatic delegated authority, and no evidence of an operational registry (source: 2026-11-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md).
  - **Effect on earlier entries**: this verifies the formal Riigikogu introduction that the 2026-11-13 digest and the 2026-11-12 section of [[estonia-ai-agent-id]] recorded as unverified. It is consistent with, and does not overturn, the 2026-10-25 agendikood position or the 2026-09-08 isikukood correction.

No other qualifying, non-duplicate Estonia source was verified (source: 2026-11-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

## Section 2 — Other countries

- **Singapore — government priorities for interoperable agent identity and mandates (17 Nov 2026; primary government speech; page hour unavailable)**: at the unveiling of MetaComp's StableX Agentic Network, Senior Minister of State Tan Kiat How named three areas for industry–government collaboration: **interoperable Know Your Agent** arrangements, **continuous assurance**, and **testing trusted transactions across organizations**. He distinguished *verifying* an agent and its accountable principal from *authorizing* particular actions, stressing spending limits, revocation and auditability. He described MAS's **SAFR** work on evaluating proposed agent transactions before execution (including identity and mandate checks) and IMDA's exploration of **DNS-based cross-platform verification** (source: 2026-11-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [MDDI — keynote by SMS Tan Kiat How](https://www.mddi.gov.sg/newsroom/keynote-address-by-sms-tan-kiat-how-at-the-unveiling-of-stablex-agentic-network/), primary).
  - **Status**: a statement of priorities — not an enacted requirement or a chosen national identity architecture. Welcoming MetaComp's industry-led network does not establish a government-operated registry. See [[singapore-ai-agent-policy]].
- **United Kingdom — DRCF joint letter on personal-agent launches (letter 16 Nov 2026, primary; secondary coverage 17 Nov)**: the CMA, FCA, ICO and Ofcom (via the DRCF) wrote to ministers that firms preparing UK personal-agent launches have engaged inconsistently with relevant regulators. The letter calls for compliance and any required authorization or registration *before* launch, continued review as products evolve, and protection of user control and connected systems; commercial partners keep their obligations when customers use agents; regulators signal readiness to enforce existing law (source: 2026-11-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.co.uk/legal/news/drcf-regulators-set-out-expectations-for-firms-launching-personal-ai-agents) and [Resultsense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-11-17-drcf-regulators-letter-ministers-personal-ai-agents/), secondary; [DRCF joint letter](https://www.drcf.org.uk/publications/joint-statements/drcf-joint-letter-personal-ai-agents), primary; publication date confirmed by the [FCA's DRCF page](https://www.fca.org.uk/about/how-we-operate/who-work-with/drcf), primary, updated 16 Nov).
  - **Status**: newly surfaced secondary coverage of a 16 Nov development, not a new in-window policy action. Distinct from the 30 Oct DRCF joint statement already on [[united-kingdom-ai-agent-policy]]. No new legislation, no universal agent-registration duty, and no named firm's breach established.

**Excluded by the report (pre-window)**: [Federal Reserve Governor Barr's financial-stability speech](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/barr20261116a.htm) (primary, 16 Nov), which discusses authentication, action-specific consent and capability limits for banking agents. It predates this window and was not in the 2026-11-16 or 2026-11-17 reports, so its content is **not otherwise recorded** in this wiki (known gap; needs verification).
