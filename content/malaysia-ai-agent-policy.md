# Malaysia: Non-Human Identities and Accountability

**Summary**: Malaysian commentary (The Star, 2026-07-28) frames autonomous AI agents as "non-human identities" that need their own access rights, permissions, and a clearer legal framework for privacy, security, and accountability.

**Sources**: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, 2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

Coverage in [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/07/28/autonomy-with-accountability) frames autonomous AI agents as non-human identities that need their own access rights, permissions, and a clearer legal framework for privacy, security, and accountability (source: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md, published 2026-07-28). This is secondary reporting and analysis rather than a government proposal or standard, in contrast to [[estonia-ai-agent-id]] and [[china-ai-agent-policy]] (source: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

It reflects the wider policy discussion about agents acting on behalf of people while using [[agent-authorization-and-delegation|delegated rights]] (source: 2026-09-04-ai-agent-identity-news.md).

## 2026-11-05: Zetrix Know-Your-Agent pilot with CAICT

Zetrix and China's CAICT announced a Know-Your-Agent service and planned cross-border pilot (scoped, revocable permissions; limits on onward delegation; signed activity records), with results intended for ITU-T SG17's agentic-AI trust and identity focus group. Corporate announcement, not Malaysian government policy; issuer claims unvalidated; the report files it under China/Malaysia and the Malaysian government's role, if any, is not stated (needs verification) (source: 2026-11-06-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [Zetrix](https://www.zetrix.com/zetrix-ai-and-china-academy-of-information-and-communications-technology-unveil-know-your-agent-kya-service-to-bring-trust-and-accountability-to-ai-agents/)).
