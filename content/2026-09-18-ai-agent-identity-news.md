### ITU and World Bank launch Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI (TIDA)

The [International Telecommunication Union](https://www.itu.int/hub/2026/09/building-trust-into-the-next-generation-of-digital-services/) announced a new Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI (TIDA), with World Bank Group participation, published 17 September 2026 (primary international standards/digital-development source). The group's planned scope covers terminology, use cases, identity models, credential formats, assurance levels, lifecycle controls, and mechanisms for government systems to verify an agent's authorization before it acts (source: 2026-09-18-ai-agent-identity-news.md, citing [ITU](https://www.itu.int/hub/2026/09/building-trust-into-the-next-generation-of-digital-services/), 2026-09-17).

Notably, the ITU frames an agent credential as evidence linking the agent to the delegating person or organization, specifying permitted capabilities and mandate duration — calling for delegation that is specific, time-bound, traceable, and revocable, with auditable evidence per action. This treats agent identity as a **trust chain** (person/organization → delegation → agent → action) rather than a standalone legal identity for the agent, and aims at mutual recognition of agent authorization across agencies and borders. See [[agent-standards-and-interoperability]] and [[agent-authorization-and-delegation]].
